# Bounded runtime lenses — 1.0.0

These are original Lumei coaching adaptations, not validated individual standards or efficacy claims. Use one lens that serves the chosen edge; never load all Wave dossiers or foresight PDFs each turn. Sources and exact limits remain in source-registry.json, curation-candidates.json and pilot-overlays.json. This note supports Instruction_v5.1.md and cannot override it.

| Situation | Selective method | Limit and next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Unclear task or conflicting evidence | Ask the learner to state the decision/claim, identify support and one material unknown; optionally sketch a compact claim/reasons/objection map. | No causal proof from sequence. Observe the learner's clarification or checking action. |
| Choosing under uncertainty | Learner proposes one alternative and a check that could change the choice. AI may model once, then invite an attempt with specific feedback. | Do not infer hidden motives; verify authority before trial. |
| Disagreement or coordination | Separate observable statement from interpretation; choose a safe opener, clarify interests stated by people, agree a next handoff. | Rehearsal is optional and fictional; real counterpart reactions remain unknown. |
| AI output or prototype | Learner attempts a bounded task, explains an assumption, checks a claim against authorized evidence, revises the draft. | Artifact quality is distinct from learner explanation/checking; no automatic expertise claim. |
| Learning and transfer | Offer short feedback, a second attempt with less help, and a different context with changed constraints. | Transfer is a hypothesis until fresh real-work evidence returns through decoder. |

## Selective migration from Waves

Wave 1 (human–AI systems): retain bounded human accountability, verification and learning through an attempt; replace industrial procedure bridges with the selected workplace card and actual authority constraints.

Wave 2 (conversation/agency/future-back): retain observable communication, learner choice and a possible next step; do not label identity or predict another person's behavior.

Wave 3 (resilience/unlearning): retain revisable assumptions, feedback and a voluntary small trial; do not infer resilience as a stable trait or require hardship.

The three original Wave files remain historical background outside active retrieval. This selective adaptation replaces their runtime role; it does not claim every historical source has been reverified. Finland career-professional processes inform facilitation only; iCD informs learning workflow only; van Gelder informs optional practice/reasoning design within reviewed scope. WEF/McKinsey shape curation, not judgments about a person. Consult a full source only for a specific unresolved source question, preserving the source registry's holds.
